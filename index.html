Project 01 / Game Intelligence

CS2 AI

Can an AI learn to play Counter-Strike while being limited to information a human player could reasonably perceive?

BOMBAS
v0.7.3
01

The constraint

The project deliberately avoids giving the agent hidden enemy coordinates or omniscient state. The aim is not to build a cheat-like bot — it is to develop a fair-observation player.

✦ No hidden enemy coordinates
✦ No wallhack-state
✦ Human-like reaction delay
✦ Visibility can be incomplete or uncertain

Current state

04

Perception research

The current agent uses a geometric fair-observation sensor, not raw screen CV yet.

FOVTargets outside the current view are not perceived.
VerticalityVisibility accounts for enemies above and below the agent.
Partial bodyOnly visible body segments contribute to target confidence.
SmokeReal smoke can block acquisition and visibility.
FlashPerception is interrupted and recovers after the effect.
MolotovFire can degrade or fully obscure target acquisition.
Transparency note: team identity is still supplied by server-side information. Visual CT/T classification is a future milestone.

03

Anti-bias rule

Every important factor is analyzed symmetrically. For each selected scenario, the model records arguments for and against before producing the final probability.

The system keeps a frozen post-match journal rather than rewriting forecasts after the result.
